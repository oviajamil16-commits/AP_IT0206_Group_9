## 3. Team Commitments

Per the assessment's AI Usage Policy, the team confirms it will:

- Read and understand every AI-generated or AI-reviewed line before submission,
  rather than submitting anything unread.
- Be able to explain and defend any part of the codebase, AI-assisted or not,
  during the live presentation and Q&A.
- Not represent AI-generated analysis (e.g. test coverage numbers, code review
  findings) as independently verified fact without the team having actually run
  the described commands and confirmed the results themselves.
- Treat AI output as a tool and a second opinion, not as a substitute for the
  team's own understanding of the system.
